PLACEHOLDER: water_running.mp3

This file should be replaced with an actual running water sound effect.

Requirements:
- Format: MP3
- Duration: Loop-friendly (seamless loop recommended)
- Content: Sound of running water, stream, or fountain
- Recommended length: 10-30 seconds (will loop)

The sound will play spatially in 3D when the user approaches A Fata character.
Volume and distance falloff are configured in AR/data/characters.json.
